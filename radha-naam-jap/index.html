Naam Jap

Radha Naam Jap

राधा नाम जाप is a devotional practice of remembering Radha’s name with attention and sincerity. A few quiet minutes can be a meaningful way to begin or close the day.

How to begin

  1. Choose a comfortable, unhurried moment.
  2. Set an intention and repeat a name or mantra you follow.
  3. Use a mala or a counter to stay present, rather than to rush.
  4. End with a moment of gratitude.

One round, 108 repetitions

A 108-bead mala is a familiar aid for counting jap. A digital counter can help you keep track when a physical mala is not with you; it is a practical tool, not a replacement for the personal meaning of traditional practice.

Make it a daily rhythm

Start with a realistic number of repetitions. Consistency and attention matter more than reaching a particular count.

Questions people ask

What is Radha Naam Jap?

It is the devotional repetition or remembrance of Radha’s name, practiced according to one’s own tradition and guidance.

Can I use a digital counter for jap?

Yes. It can help record repetitions, while the focus remains on mindful remembrance.
