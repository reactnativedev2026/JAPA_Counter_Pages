Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Parihar Tech ("we", "us", "our") builds and supports software applications, including apps distributed through the Google Play Store. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

Who we are

Parihar Tech is a Udyam-registered Micro enterprise (Reg. No. UDYAM-MP-30-0070031) based in Mandsaur, Madhya Pradesh, India, proprietor Govind Singh.

Information we collect

The specific data collected depends on the app you're using. In general, we may collect:

  • Account information — name, email address, and phone number, if you register or contact us.
  • Usage data — how you interact with the app, such as screens viewed and features used, to help us fix bugs and improve the product.
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, and app version, for troubleshooting and compatibility.
  • Content you provide — information you enter directly into the app, such as support requests or feedback.

We do not collect more than an app needs to function, and each individual app's Play Store listing will describe its specific data collection in the Data Safety section.

How we use information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core functionality
  • To respond to support requests and communicate with you
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve reliability
  • To meet legal or regulatory obligations, where applicable

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the app (such as hosting or analytics), bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Law enforcement or regulators, only where required by applicable law

Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below. You may also uninstall the app at any time to stop further data collection by it.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will remove it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

This policy applies to the app(s) published on the Google Play Store by Parihar Tech. If a specific app requires additional or different disclosures, those will be listed on that app's Play Store page under Data Safety.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
