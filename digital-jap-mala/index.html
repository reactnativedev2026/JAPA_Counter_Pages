Digital Jap Mala

A digital jap mala is a simple counting companion for moments when you want to practice but do not have a physical mala nearby.

Traditional mala and digital support

A physical mala carries personal and traditional significance. A digital tool does not claim to be the same thing; it can simply help keep count of repetitions and support a regular routine.

Using a 108-bead rhythm

Choose a name or mantra, settle your attention, and tap for each repetition. You can complete a full round of 108 or use a smaller count that fits your day.
