Festival guide

Mahashivratri

Mahashivratri is observed in diverse ways across Hindu communities. This evergreen page focuses on its devotional meaning rather than a date, which changes by year and location.

Significance

Mahashivratri is a significant Shiva observance, often marked by worship, vigil and mantra remembrance.

Devotional practice

Families and communities observe this occasion in different ways. Reading, quiet Naam Jap, Aarti and charitable acts may be meaningful where they align with your tradition. Confirm year-specific timings with a trusted local Panchang.
