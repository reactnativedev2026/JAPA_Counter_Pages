Counting with attention

Radha Naam Jap Counter

A Naam Jap Counter gives each repetition a simple place to land. It is useful for people who wish to keep an accurate count during Radha Naam Jap, Krishna Naam Jap or mantra jap.

How a digital jap counter works

Tap once for each repetition. A count can help structure a round of 108 without interrupting your practice to remember the number. The app’s counter is paired with devotional content, so your reading and jap can live in one place.

For a quieter routine

Use the counter at a pace that feels respectful. It is not a measure of devotion; it is a small aid for regular practice.
