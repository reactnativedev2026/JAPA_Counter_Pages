Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 6

Dhyana Yoga

A practical vision of a steady, disciplined mind.

Chapter 6 summary

In Dhyana Yoga, the teaching turns to the questions raised by the setting of the Gita. The chapter invites reflective reading: notice the relationship between intention, conduct and a steadier understanding of the self. Its ideas become clearer when read within the sequence of all eighteen chapters.

Main themes

  • Steady practice
  • Balance in daily life
  • Meditation and attention
